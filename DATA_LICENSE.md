## Derived data: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

The following are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
licence:

- **LLMCuisine-22 (D1)**: the LLM recipes with ingredients aliased to CulinaryDB entities, in CulinaryDB's file format;
- the flavor profiles and shared-compound matrices built from FlavorDB;
- every table derived from CulinaryDB recipes.

These are adaptations of material under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, and share-alike licences allow a later version.

Required attribution:

- **CulinaryDB**: Singh, N. & Bagler, G. (2018). *Data-driven investigations of culinary patterns in traditional
  recipes across the world.* IEEE ICDE Workshops. CoSyLab, IIIT-Delhi, https://cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/culinarydb/
  (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
- **FlavorDB**: Garg, N. et al. (2018). *FlavorDB: a database of flavor molecules.* Nucleic Acids Research 46,
  D1210–D1216. CoSyLab, IIIT-Delhi, https://cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/flavordb/ (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
- **Ahn et al. (2011)**, *Flavor network and the principles of food pairing*, Scientific Reports 1:196. The data is
  on Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.11449657, under **CC BY 4.0**; we use it for four flavor profiles missing from
  FlavorDB.

Changes we made: ingredient aliasing of LLM text with our implementation of the CulinaryDB protocol; reconstructed
flavor profiles for the 24 CulinaryDB ingredients absent from FlavorDB (documented in
`src/flavoraudit/flavor.py`); derived statistics.
